# EcoTern Summer Bootcamp 2026 — AI and Machine Learning for Earth and Environmental Science

A five-day intensive training program designed to introduce undergraduate students to the foundations of AI and its applications in environmental and Earth system science. Through invited industry talks, PI-led tutorials, and hands-on labs, participants explore core AI concepts and practical workflows for real-world environmental challenges.
